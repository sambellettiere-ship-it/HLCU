About Me

Meet the Founder,
Muhammad

I'm Muhammad, the guy behind Hidden Level. I opened this place because Urbana didn't have a spot where gaming felt like a community thing instead of a solo thing — somewhere you could drop in, play, and know the people next to you.

Before this, my background was in coaching and movement: 7 years teaching Taekwondo, a few years in boxing, and a degree in exercise science. My current job is as a PE teacher, which helps me support this business. I spent years helping people build skill and discipline in a physical space — now I'm doing the same thing here, just with controllers instead of pads.

I always loved the fitness environments and the cultures that come with them — that sense of support, encouragement, and brotherhood — and I always wished for something like it, but for gamers. Whether it's running our weekly community events, organizing tournament nights, or just making sure the PCs run right, I care about building something people actually want to come back to.

🕹️

About the Business

More Than a Café.
A Community Hub.

Hidden Level Cyber Cafe is Urbana-Champaign's gaming destination, located on University Ave in Urbana, IL. Whether you're a hardcore gamer or just looking for a fun night out, we have something for everyone.

Our café features PC gaming stations, a console lounge with PlayStation 5 and Nintendo setups, and a comfortable TV lounge — all backed by top-tier networking infrastructure for a seamless gaming experience.

Beyond the games, Hidden Level is a community hub. We host regular community events that bring locals together for shared gaming experiences, friendly competition, and good vibes.

We also offer private bookings and monthly memberships for regulars who want to make us their home base.
