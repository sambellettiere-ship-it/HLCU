The Premier Destination for PC & Console Gaming

Whether you are on the hunt for a competitive gaming center in Champaign, IL or looking for a casual high-tech cyber cafe in Urbana, IL, Hidden Level is the space you've been looking for. Strategically situated at 110 E University Ave, Suite I, Urbana IL, we sit right on the crossroad connecting both vibrant communities.

Our facility is engineered to deliver zero-compromise gaming excellence. Designed and managed by local gaming veterans, we understand what makes a perfect LAN hub—from ultra-stable high-speed fiber internet and custom-built PC rigs to comfortable gaming desk configurations and immersive atmosphere.

Serving Local Communities

Our Champaign-Urbana Gaming Footprint

As the twin cities grew, finding a dedicated spot for public PC gaming and real physical community became challenging. Hidden Level fills that void in Champaign and Urbana by creating an safe, inviting, and state-of-the-art cafe space.

Our proximity to UIUC, Savoy, and local Illinois schools allows us to provide gaming access to people from all walks of life. Whether you want to form a collegiate team, practice for national esports tournaments, or just experience local split-screen fun, we welcome you.

We work closely with local groups, student clubs, and tech organizations to host LAN actions, birthday parties, and custom corporate bookings. Connect with us on our Contact page to set up your next event!

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to commonly asked questions about our gaming center and cyber cafe.

📍 Where is the gaming center located?

Hidden Level is proudly situated at 110 E University Ave, Suite I, Urbana, IL 61801. We are in the heart of East Illinois, right off University Ave, making it a quick commute for anyone coming from Urbana, Champaign, Savoy, or the UIUC campus. We also have parking spaces.

🕒 What are the operational business hours?

We are open Tuesday through Saturday from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays and Sundays. Keep an eye on our Events Calendar for tournaments and holiday schedule changes!

💵 How much does it cost to play at the cyber cafe?

A simple walk-in Day Pass or Event Entry fee is only $15 per person! It grants access to top-of-the-line setups, PCs, and our signature events. If you're a regular, check out our monthly membership packages starting at $50/month which offer incredible play rates and cafe perks. Let's look at the Pricing page for details.

💻 What games are pre-installed on the PC rigs?

We keep all top competitive multiplayer and co-op games fully updated in our library, including Fortnite, Valorant, League of Legends, Apex Legends, Overwatch, Minecraft, Rocket League, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate on active consoles, and multiple retro games. You can also log into your personal game launchers to access your own libraries!
