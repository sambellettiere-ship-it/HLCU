FAQ

Cyber Cafe & Gaming Cafe Questions

Everything you need to know before you drop in.

🌐 Is there a cyber cafe or internet cafe near me in Champaign-Urbana?

Yes — Hidden Level is a cyber cafe and internet cafe at 110 E University Ave, Suite I, Urbana, IL, right on the Champaign-Urbana border and minutes from the UIUC campus. A $15 day pass gets you high-speed internet access, a gaming PC or workstation, and free Wi-Fi. We also serve Savoy, Tolono, Mahomet, and St. Joseph.

🕒 What are your hours?

We're open Tuesday–Saturday, 4:00–10:00 PM, and closed Sundays and Mondays. Check the events calendar for tournaments and holiday changes.

💵 How much does it cost to play?

A walk-in day pass or event entry is $15 per person. Regulars can join a monthly membership from $70/month, and private bookings for up to 20 guests are available. See the pricing page for details.

🎮 What can I play?

High-performance gaming PCs with NVIDIA RTX graphics, PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch consoles, retro titles, and a TV lounge — Fortnite, Valorant, League of Legends, Rocket League, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, and your own libraries via personal launchers.
